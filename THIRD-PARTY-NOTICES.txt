USBPcap 1.5.4.0
--------------
USBPcap is an independent third-party USB capture driver by Tomasz Mon and
contributors. Its driver is GPL-2.0; USBPcapCMD has a BSD-2-Clause license.
The app uses USBPcap's documented capture interface. It does not contain a
copy of USBPcap's driver implementation.

USBPcap binaries are NOT included in this app's installer or source archive.
If the driver is missing, setup offers a consent-based download of the
unchanged official installer, verified against a pinned SHA-256 digest, and
launches it visibly so users can review and accept USBPcap's own license
screens. The setup does not silently accept or bypass those licenses.
USBPcap retains its own licenses and is not relicensed under this app's MIT
license. Uninstalling this app does not uninstall USBPcap.

Official project: https://desowin.org/usbpcap/
Release: https://github.com/desowin/usbpcap/releases/tag/1.5.4.0
Matching source:
https://github.com/desowin/usbpcap/releases/download/1.5.4.0/usbpcap-src-incl-pdb-1.5.4.0.7z
